Flurry the snowman: build him in Blender with AI

What this does: the exact prompt behind the "Make a snowman in Blender" Short. Your AI writes one Blender Python script that builds Flurry from simple shapes, with the same sizes and numbers shown in the video.

You need: Blender 4.x or 5.x and an AI (Claude, Antigravity or similar).

  • Easy: paste the prompt into your AI, copy the script it writes, then in Blender open the Scripting tab, press New, paste, and press the Run button (Alt+P).
  • Connected: if your AI is connected to Blender (MCP), tell it to run the script for you. Set that up with the Blender + MCP setup page.
Save your Blender file first. Scripts written by an AI can change or delete things in your scene, and the first version is rarely perfect.

The prompt (copy everything below)

You are an expert Blender Python (bpy) artist. Write ONE complete script for Blender 4.x or 5.x that builds
"Flurry", a cute snowman mascot, from simple primitives. Use only bpy, bmesh and math. Start by clearing the scene.
All sizes are in Blender units, the floor is at z = 0.

Body (UV spheres, 64 segments, 32 rings, smooth shading):
- Bottom: radius 1.0 at (0, 0, 1.0)
- Belly: radius 0.75 at (0, 0, 2.5)
- Head: radius 0.6 at (0, 0, 3.7)
Snow material on all three: Principled BSDF, base color (0.88, 0.94, 1.0), roughness 0.6, subsurface weight 0.3,
subsurface radius (0.4, 0.6, 1.0), plus a Noise texture (scale 80, detail 6) into a Bump node (strength 0.05) on the normal.

Face (on the -Y side of the head):
- Eyes: two glossy black spheres, radius 0.1, at (-0.2, -0.53, 3.8) and (0.2, -0.53, 3.8), each with a tiny white
  emissive sparkle sphere (radius 0.03) at x + 0.035, y = -0.615, z = 3.84.
- Cheeks: two pink flattened spheres, radius 0.1, scale (1, 0.3, 0.7), at (+-0.36, -0.47, 3.62).
- Nose: a carrot-orange cone, base radius 0.1, depth 0.5, rotated 90 degrees on X so it points to -Y, at (0, -0.76, 3.68).
- Smile: seven small black spheres (radius 0.03) on an arc from x = -0.24 to 0.24, z = 3.5 + 0.08 * t * t
  (t from -1 to 1), placed on the head surface around y = -0.45.

Clothes:
- Scarf: a yellow torus (major radius 0.5, minor radius 0.13) at (0, 0, 3.08), plus a flat yellow tail
  (a box scaled 0.2 x 0.07 x 0.6) at (0.3, -0.7, 2.62).
- Buttons: three small spheres, radius 0.07, on the front of the belly at z = 2.85, 2.5 and 2.15 (the middle one yellow).
- Top hat: black cylinder, radius 0.32, depth 0.5, at z = 4.52. Brim: cylinder, radius 0.5, depth 0.05, at z = 4.27.
  Red band: cylinder, radius 0.325, depth 0.12, at z = 4.35. Add glowing yellow text "3D" (size 0.16) on the front of the band.
- Arms: two thin brown cylinders (radius 0.06, depth 1.2) at (+-1.25, 0, 2.85), rotated on Y by 90 degrees +- 40 degrees.

Scene:
- Dark blue world color (0.03, 0.06, 0.16), a pale snow-blue floor plane, a cool sun light (energy 2.2),
  a warm key area light at (3, -6, 4) with energy 900, and a cool fill area light at (-4, 3, 3) with energy 300.
- Camera at (0, -15.5, 2.5) looking along +Y, 50 mm lens, framing the whole snowman.
- Render with EEVEE and AgX, 1080 x 1920 (vertical), one still image saved to the folder I give you.

Parent every part to one empty called "FlurryRoot" and name every object clearly.
Comment each section and tell me in 3 short lines how to run it.

Make it yours (ask for one change at a time)

  • "Make the scarf red and the hat green."
  • "Make the carrot nose 20% longer."
  • "Add small snowballs on the ground."
